Decisions In This Build
Foundation: white surfaces, #F2F2F2 / #E5E5E5 structure, #333 and #8C8C8C type. Chestnut #995A00 is reserved for emphasis only — active chip, rank 1, your own row, active nav.
Home: auto-looping banner carousel (4s, tap the dots), welcome line with the logged-in name, rank/points tiles and four quick links. No manufacturer chips here — those live on the module screens.
Profile: reached from the hex initials in the header — name, registered number, trade and district, then Log Out and a destructive Delete Account.
Podium: amber gradient panel behind the top three, with gold, silver and bronze medals replacing the gift badge on each hexagon.
Points card: collapsible “How You Earn Points” strip under the chips — base rate in the header line, premium SKU multipliers on expand, per manufacturer.
My Demands: a second tab inside the Demand module (New Demand / My Demands) rather than a fifth nav item — count tiles, status filters, and one card per demand across all manufacturers. Green #006600 marks allocated demands only.
Demand: the honeycomb trail is sticky and shows your choices. Missing manufacturer is an info banner followed by the SKU list.
