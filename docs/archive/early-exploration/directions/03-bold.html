Direction 03 of 04

Bold

Built for the worst conditions the product will actually meet: bright sun, a scratched screen, a user who reads slowly, and hands that are not clean.

No cards, no shadows, no soft greys. Full-bleed colour blocks divided by 3 px black rules. Figures at 74 px. Buttons 60 dp tall and uppercase. Every element is either black, brand brown, amber, or paper — nothing in between.

Motion signature
Blocks wipe in horizontally rather than fading. Figures pop with a slight overshoot. Tab selection sweeps amber up from the bottom edge. Detail screens sweep in from the right.
Best for
Legibility and confidence. This is the most accessible of the four by a wide margin, and the cheapest to render.
Risk
Shouty at length. Dense screens — the points ledger, a long allocation list — get tiring, and there is little room for nuance in status.
