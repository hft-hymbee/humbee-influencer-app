Stack

Physical, layered, tactile. Content sits on cards that stack, lift and settle — so a list of allocations reads as a pile of receipts you can thumb through rather than rows in a database.

The brand brown becomes a deep gradient on the hero surface; everything else stays white and floats above a warm ground. Depth carries the hierarchy that the Ledger direction gets from rules.

Motion signature
Spring easing with overshoot on every entrance. Cards lift on hover and compress on press. The reward stepper pulses at its live stage. Sheets rise from the bottom edge.
Best for
Familiarity. This is the visual language of every payments app on the target user's phone, so it costs them nothing to learn.
Risk
Shadows and gradients cost fill-rate on 2 GB devices, and soft grey-on-white loses contrast in direct sunlight.
