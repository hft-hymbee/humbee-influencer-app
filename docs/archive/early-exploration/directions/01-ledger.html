Ledger

A passbook, not an app. The product's job is to convince a barbender that a number is true — so this direction borrows from the thing he already trusts: a ruled account book.

No cards, no shadows, no rounded corners. Hairline rules divide, heavy rules anchor. Every figure is tabular and oversized. Colour is spent almost entirely on the role tag, because that is the one piece of information most easily lost.

Motion signature
Figures count up on arrival. Entries stagger in from below, 40 ms apart. Bars grow from the baseline. The tab rule draws across rather than fading.
Best for
Trust, verification, dispute. Reads well in sunlight and survives a cheap panel.
Risk
Austere. Umang Utsav and lucky draws have to carry their own warmth, and this direction gives them little help.
