Celebrate

The one direction that treats recognition as the product rather than a feature of it. Progress rings, milestone badges, streaks, and a full-screen moment when a reward lands.

The argument for it: Humbee's whole pitch to an influencer is being seen — for volume, for loyalty, for turning up at Umang Utsav. A ledger records that. This celebrates it.

Try it: open Rewards and tap "I've received this" on the refrigerator to see the win moment.
Motion signature
SVG rings draw on arrival. Badges pop in sequence. Streak bars fill from the base. The reward moment lands a trophy with a spring and fires a confetti burst on canvas.
Best for
Retention and the emotional half of the brief — anniversaries, milestones, Umang Utsav, the leaderboard.
Risk
The heaviest of the four to render, and celebration sits awkwardly next to a disputed allocation. Needs a restrained mode for anything involving money going wrong.
